## 用处 | Potential Applications

本协议可用于尝试以下类型的 AI 运算：

- **问题重构：**重新检验问题本身及其成立条件，而非直接接受提问者设定的前提。
- **多主体分析：**识别不同主体的原点、行为、关系、利益及代价承担。
- **动态推演：**考虑主体、原点、现实条件及外部框架随事件发展的变化。
- **决策辅助：**分析判断依据、潜在影响、主体承载能力及有效边界。
- **逻辑检验：**检验局部合理性、整体合理性以及判断依据之间的关系。
- **持续可校：**使既有结论、运算过程乃至运算规则自身重新进入检验。
- **运算接续：**通过停止位置、停止原因及补入信息记录，探索跨时间、跨使用者及跨模型的运算接续。
- **AI 技术研究：**探索五端相互限权、动态牵动及持续可校关系的形式化与工程实现。

上述为原型协议的潜在用途及研究方向，不代表相关能力已经通过独立验证，亦不构成其完整适用范围。

## English overview (informative)

Origin Theory AI Protocol, Prototype 0.1, is a natural-language reasoning protocol that uses a five-part mutually constraining operational structure. Its aim is to make premises, subjects, relationships, costs, boundaries and judgments open to examination and revision, rather than to replace real-world decision-makers.

The Simplified Chinese protocol is the reference text. This repository is a specification prototype, not a runnable engine or a validated AI architecture. Use the complete, version-pinned document for experiments; distinguish original text, derivative interpretations and third-party implementations. No performance or conformance claim is made.

Potential research applications include problem reframing, multi-subject analysis, dynamic reasoning, decision support, examination of premises and judgments, revisable reasoning, and investigation of resumable reasoning and formal implementation. These are proposed uses, not validated capabilities. The protocol addresses AI reasoning rather than control of human behavior. Model outputs do not represent the author’s judgments or the complete theory.
